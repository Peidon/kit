Form Bot
======
It's a Browser extension, help autofill web forms.

How to use it ?
--
<p align="center">
<img src="autofill-extension/icons/panel.png" width=auto height="200" />
</p>

Open your browser’s extensions page, click `Load unpacked`, and select the `autofill-extension` directory. After that, you’ll see the extension icon in the toolbar. Click the icon, and a panel will pop up.   

There are three buttons on the panel.  

**[ Autofill ]** : Autofill the forms in any web page.  
**[ Learn ]** : The first time you use Form Bot, it needs to learn the information you enter. After you complete a form, simply click **[ Learn ]**, and the same fields can be autofilled automatically next time.  
**[ Review ]** : Sometimes, you may want to update or delete a field value. You can organize and refine the information you’ve entered — keeping it clean and precise will make the autofill more accurate.
